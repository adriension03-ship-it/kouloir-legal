Terms of Use

Version 1 · effective November 10, 2026 · The French version prevails in case of difference.

Zero tolerance. Kouloir accepts no objectionable content and no abusive users: harassment, threats, hate, discrimination, sexual content, sharing personal information or attacking a named person. Such content is removed and its author may be excluded, without notice.

1. Who we are

Kouloir is a mobile app where students of the same school post short messages, anonymously or under a username, and vote on other people's posts. It is published by Adrien Sion (see the legal notice). Contact: contact@kouloir.fr.

2. Accepting these terms

By creating an account and ticking the box, you accept these terms and the privacy policy. If you don't accept them, don't use Kouloir.

3. Who can use Kouloir

  • You must be 17 or older.
  • You need a valid school email address (for HEC Paris: an @hec.edu address). It is only used to check that you belong to the school.
  • One account per person. Your account is personal: don't lend it.

4. Anonymity, and its limits

  • When you post anonymously, other students only see an alias (for example “Violet 7”). They can't know who you are.
  • Anonymity applies between users. Every piece of content stays linked to your account in our systems. Kouloir's admins can see the account behind reported content, only to moderate it.
  • If a judicial authority asks, we must hand over the data the law requires us to keep. Being anonymous on Kouloir does not protect you from prosecution for illegal content.

5. What is not allowed

You may not post, in a post, reply, image, meme, poll or username:

  • harassment, threats or intimidation;
  • content that names, points to or targets an identifiable person, even indirectly;
  • hateful or discriminatory content (origin, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability…);
  • sexual or pornographic content, or anything involving a minor;
  • personal information: full name, phone number, address, email, a photo of someone without their consent;
  • glorification of violence, terrorism, Nazism, suicide or self-harm;
  • any illegal content, or content that infringes other people's rights (copyright, image rights, privacy);
  • spam, advertising, or impersonating someone.

You may also not try to find out who an anonymous author is, get around an exclusion, or disrupt the service.

6. Moderation

  • Filter: a list of banned words blocks some posts before they are sent.
  • Reporting: anyone can report a post or reply with the “•••” button. Reports are anonymous. Admins see every reported item.
  • Freeze: at 15 reports, content is removed from the feed until an admin decides. An admin can also freeze it at any time.
  • Decision: admins handle reports within 24 hours. They can restore content, delete it permanently, require a username change, suspend or delete an account.
  • These decisions are at our sole discretion, based on these terms.

7. Blocking a user

From the “•••” button, you can block the author of a piece of content: you no longer see their content, and they are not told. To protect anonymity, blocking from anonymous content hides their anonymous content; blocking from content posted under a username hides their content under that username. Your blocked list is in Settings, where you can unblock.

  • You grant us a free, worldwide licence to host, display and reproduce your content in Kouloir, for as long as it is published there.
  • You can delete your posts and replies at any time.
  • Only post images you have the right to share.

9. Deleting your account

In Settings › Delete my account. Deletion is immediate and permanent: your account, posts, replies, votes and notifications are erased.
