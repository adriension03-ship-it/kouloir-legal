Privacy Policy

Version 1 · effective November 10, 2026 · The French version prevails in case of difference.

In short. We collect the minimum: your school email to check you belong there, and what you post. No ads, no tracking, no data sold. Other students never see your email, or who wrote an anonymous post.

1. Data controller

Adrien Sion, publisher of Kouloir (see the legal notice). For any question or request: contact@kouloir.fr.

2. The data we process

We don't collect your location, your contacts or any advertising identifier. Kouloir has no advertising or analytics tools.

3. Anonymity

  • Other students never see your email. For anonymous content, they only see an alias.
  • In our systems, every piece of content stays linked to your account. Kouloir's admins can see the account behind reported content, only to moderate it.
  • Nobody sees who reported content or who blocked whom.

4. Who receives this data

  • Kouloir's admins (the founders), for moderation.
  • Our technical providers, acting on our instructions:
    • Supabase: database, accounts and images, hosted in the European Union;
    • Resend (United States): sending the email with your sign-in code;
    • Expo (United States) and Apple: delivering push notifications.
  • Judicial authorities, upon lawful request.

Transfers outside the European Union rely on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

Images posted in Kouloir are stored under a random name. Anyone with their exact address can open them: don't post private images.

5. How long

  • Your account and content: until you delete them, or delete your account.
  • Content removed by moderation: erased; only a record of the decision is kept, without the text.
  • Notifications: shown for 30 days, erased with your account.
  • Technical connection data: the retention period of our technical logs, up to one year.

Deleting your account (Settings › Delete my account) immediately erases your account, posts, replies, votes, reports, blocks and notifications.

6. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, erase or port your data, or object to its processing, by writing to contact@kouloir.fr. We answer within one month. If you think your rights are not respected, you can complain to the CNIL, the French data protection authority (cnil.fr).

7. Age

Kouloir is for people aged 17 and over.

8. Security

Traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Access to data is restricted by server-side rules: the app never receives the identity of the author of anonymous content.

9. Changes

If this policy changes significantly, the app will tell you and ask for your agreement again.
